ABOUT THIS GAME
---------------
You are Living_Sin—an operative with no memory and a lot of questions. Drop onto the Ringworld, an ancient megastructure that was supposed to be an ark. It's not. It's a prison, and something broke the seals.

Ringworld Redux is a full-campaign Halo-inspired FPS built around:
- 60Hz tactical gameplay with no frame dragging
- Real US military weapons: M4A1, M16A4, M110, M40A5, M249, M1014, M17/M9A1, M136 AT4, MP7A1
- 3-slot loadout: Main, Secondary, Space
- Human explosives: M67 Grenade, M18 Claymore, M15 Anti-Tank Mine
- Alien arsenal: Seeker Bots, Alien Mines, Sentry Bots
- Theoretical space weapons: Plasma Rifle, Particle Cannon, Gauss Gun, Laser Pistol, Railgun
- Legend difficulty: unrestricted human and alien AI with full squad tactics
- Oracle-inspired AI constructs with dark faction color patterns
- 4th-wall narrative that knows it's a game

STORY
-----
Oracle Prime makes contact. Oracle Shade mocks your loadout. Lia comments on your collision detection. The ringworld is a weapon, a prison, and someone forgot to patch the leaks.
